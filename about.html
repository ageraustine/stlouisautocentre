Our story

Trinity Auto Garage to St. Louis Auto Centre

St. Louis Auto Centre Ltd started as Trinity Auto Garage in 2014 and was incorporated in January 2023 under the laws of Kenya as a Motor Garage and Vehicle Sales Enterprise.

In a short time we have served a varied clientele, including government, individual and insurance clients. We are run by qualified, dedicated and self-driven professionals with a goal-oriented aim of providing quality service, and we bring wide experience in motor vehicle repair and maintenance.

Elizabeth Wangari
Director
