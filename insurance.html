How it works

From accident to handover

  1. 01

    Bring your vehicle

    Visit us, or call to arrange recovery of your vehicle to our workshop.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    We inspect the damage and prepare the repair details your insurer needs.

  3. 03

    Approval

    Once your insurer approves the repair, we get to work.

  4. 04

    Repair & finish

    Our team repairs, paints and checks the vehicle against our quality standard.

  5. 05

    Handover

    We hand back your vehicle and walk you through the work done.
