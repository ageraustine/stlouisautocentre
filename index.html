About us

From a small garage to a trusted name

St. Louis Auto Centre Ltd started as Trinity Auto Garage in 2014 and was incorporated in January 2023 as a Motor Garage and Vehicle Sales Enterprise. In a short time we have served a wide clientele, including government, individual and insurance clients.

We are run by qualified, dedicated and self-driven professionals with a goal-oriented focus on quality service and wide experience in motor vehicle repair and maintenance.

Elizabeth Wangari
Director
